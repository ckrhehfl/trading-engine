## First diagnostic: EM-C-ATTRIBUTION-1

Read the saved C ledgers for onset and confirmation controls, both original
entry models, and original fixed/full-forecast/half-forecast management. Keep
their exact requests, quantities, cost, structural exits and expiry. Model
selection controls are not silently introduced to rescue a negative arm.

Report the complete event funnel, including absent/late/invalid confirmation,
unkeyed no-commit decisions, committed orders, known no-fills, unknown fills,
settled fills and unknown cash. Preserve unknowns rather than zeroing them.
Reconstruct exact settled cash from purchase, sale quantities and prices and
the existing cost; do not merely trust stored net totals.

Comparisons are paired by the original episode:

- Onset versus confirmation: whole original roster and common settled fills,
  plus changed fill/selection categories. This changes timing, confirmation
  information and entry-relative expiry; it is not a pure latency treatment.
  Any retrospectively confirmed cohort is descriptive and not an executable
  pre-onset selection rule.
- Confirmation reference limit versus capped next opening: same causal entry
  request, fixed exit. Separate both-filled, capped-only, neither and unknown.
  A difference among additionally filled names describes selection under these
  price rules, not historical queue position or certified adverse selection.
- Fixed versus forecast-full and forecast-half: same confirmation request,
  actual initial modeled price and integer quantity, with paired settled cash
  and unknown pairs explicit. Preserve entry cost once across partial sales.

For every known filled confirmation/reference-limit/fixed-exit book, inspect
its saved original minute path from the minute after entry through its first
structural or time liquidation. A missing price/receipt before this boundary
prevents an assertion that an opportunity never existed. A known positive
observation despite another gap remains an observed lower-bound occurrence,
not a complete maximum. Never extend beyond the original expiry or stop to
manufacture a rescue. No quote/depth support is inferred from an opening.

For a complete path record these exact descriptive fields:

1. Largest and smallest subsequent minute-opening return relative to the
   actual modeled purchase, and the first offset of each. These are hindsight
   price bounds, not a policy that knew when to sell.
2. Whether any such opening strictly exceeded the existing round-trip cost,
   and whether the actual final sale did. Separate never exceeded, exceeded
   and retained, and exceeded then lost. Exact equality remains distinct.
3. The first received post-entry close strictly above the same cost threshold,
   its receipt clock and the next minute opening if it occurs no later than
   the fixed structural/time exit. This is a frozen causal trigger witness,
   not use of the earlier close as a fill. Its missing/out-of-window opening
   remains explicit. A threshold witness does not select the input cohort.
4. Exit time and fixed/forecast sale reason. A difference from a hindsight
   maximum alone cannot prove that a better causal exit was available.

The diagnostic reports all fills as weekly frequency and winning fills as a
separate column. Fifteen wins per week is not a requirement. Cash is nominal
event cash, not a simultaneously funded account; overlapping events retain
their date/name/episode identity. Report date and name concentration, paired
known-subset differences, and exact unknown counts. No p-value or universal
strategy verdict follows from an unadjusted collection of ledger rows.

also considers the purchase bar's subsequently received close; excluding that

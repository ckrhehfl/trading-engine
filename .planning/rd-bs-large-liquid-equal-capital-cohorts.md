## Operator request and investigation — 2026-10-16 KST

After BR's completed one-control descriptive comparison and independent saved-
result arithmetic verification, the operator explicitly requested multiple
same-universe controls at the same investment allocation, continuing through
review, CI, normal merge, isolated GCP research deployment and actual results
without routine permission stops. This is a new descriptive comparison family;
it is not a repeat of the original immutable ACTIVITY or BR books. The previous
failed cost-floor power prerequisite remains false. The request authorizes this
bounded exploratory continuation, not a power pass, promotion, winning lag,
direction closure or reserved-window access. CLAUDE.md's Discovery guards,
historical-availability sensitivity, research input rules and review policy
remain binding. No new conclusion is written into CLAUDE.md.

Source-only investigation found that BR's counted targets do not guarantee equal
realized noncash exposure. Each book allocates the same fraction of its own
opening NAV to a new position, so different NAV levels alone are not an unequal-
weight policy. Retained positions, failed trades and price changes cause actual
exposure to diverge. Forcing equal daily closing weights would require a new
rebalance/execution/cost policy or a counterfactual attribution calculation.
The task therefore fixes equal assigned capital at each original acquisition
opportunity and removes cross-formation reinvestment differences through
independent cohorts. It must not be described as exact daily exposure matching.

Two source-only investigators independently inspected selection/replay/report
contracts without reading actual saved market, replay or report bodies. The
design audit recommended this cohort approach over daily return reweighting,
provided the allocated-budget denominator and conditional sample are explicit.
Their investigation establishes engineering feasibility, not actual inputs or
results. This document is created alongside work that has now started.

The fixed primary diagnostic is

    T_p = sum_f(final_NAV_p,f - 1) / sum_f(B_f)

and each nonempty cohort reports `(final_NAV - 1) / B_f`. The denominator is
assigned acquisition budgets, not actual filled capital. Zero-target formations
remain declared and contribute no denominator; an all-zero family has undefined
normalized return. Preserve actual entry counts/principal, component sales,
remaining inventory and noncash exposure separately.

strict-positive count and ties for each timing arm. Five assignments measure
selection sensitivity on the same market history; they do not create five
independent market samples or repair the failed power prerequisite. No p-value,
permutation significance, strategy pass or confirmation claim is produced.

If an aggregate path is reported, it is the ex-post capital-attribution curve
`1 + sum_f(NAV_p,f,t - 1) / sum_f(B_f)`. Its full-family denominator uses future
opportunity counts only for retrospective normalization, never selection or a
historical allocation decision. Label it as such, without calling it the
compounded continuous-account result. Report its return, unannualized daily

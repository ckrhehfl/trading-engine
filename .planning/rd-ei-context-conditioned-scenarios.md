## Objective and scope

future path distributions and economic value in two situations:

- A: an advance followed by its first pullback and potential resumption.
- B: an already extended advance testing a high/resistance again.

strategy. Keep other/unclassified, unknown, no-event and no-entry observations.

## Initial causal measurement decisions

The first support implementation observes every completed five-minute endpoint
E >= 30 at E+2 inside the regular session. It independently records 30/60/120
minute price and volume support and the complete same-session opening prefix.
It is not restricted to an EH opening-range breakout or a later confirmed EG
event. Current support may recover after a late row, but a first-attempt claim
cannot be retroactively recovered when an earlier required decision was unknown.

The earliest eligible confirmed same-session high is the first anchor. A
center high requires left.high < center.high >= right.high. Use the median
individual range of the three bars immediately before the center as R. Require
R > 0 and advance from the session's first open to the peak >= 2R. Freeze the
anchor when the confirming right bar is available, no earlier than E32.
The reference band is peak +/- R/4. These are initial source-design choices,
not optimized or empirically validated thresholds.

A describes its first positive-depth pullback no deeper than half the advance,
within six completed bars after the peak and at most one separate retest.
First resumption is a later completed close above the preceding bar's high;
it is a phase annotation and not an order. B requires advance >= 3R, at least
two separate observed retests and a current bar overlapping the anchor band.
A retest must follow a completed bar fully below the band; consecutive touching
bars are not multiple attempts and intrabar ordering is not inferred. A close
at or below the original starting open invalidates the anchor permanently for
this census. It is retained with its identity and never silently replaced.

This initial first-anchor scope does not represent every intraday advance.
Later advances do not create new anchors. A later return to the original
anchor band can still be B if its fixed conditions hold, even after a higher
high elsewhere in the session. Thus B is a repeated test of the original
level, not proof that it remains the current session high or resistance.
Exact depth, duration, normalized advance and retest count are retained for
a bounded, registered follow-up.
Known no-anchor or zero-reference-range is unclassified; unsupported required
history is unknown. Price classification does not require observed volume.

Context and event are separate. A pullback frame and B resistance-touch frame
are not interchangeable trade opportunities. Before the future snapshot, freeze
a common approach/break/no-event definition and compare context-free and
context-conditioned models on exactly those same events, targets and support.
Support census frames alone cannot establish conditional entry superiority.

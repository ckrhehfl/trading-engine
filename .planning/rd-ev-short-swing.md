## Paired policies

entry session, an exit before the end of the next session, and a conditional
one-night hold. Write L for the exclusive continuous-market endpoint, ten

conflicting evidence stays unknown, including any unknown sale proceeds.
Keep a known structural sale even when the subsequent price path is incomplete.
Do not infer a halt, corporate action or an executable trade from an empty bar.

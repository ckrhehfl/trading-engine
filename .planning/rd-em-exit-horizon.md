## Comparison 1: cost-close response within original thirty minutes

For the same original entries, retain the original thirty-minute expiry and
structural stop. Starting with the purchase bar, process completed closes only
at their admitted receipt clocks. The first received close strictly greater
than purchase price times (1 + 33/10000) schedules a full sale at the next
minute opening. Exact equality does not trigger. The triggering close is never
used as a sale price. A scheduled structural/time liquidation takes precedence;
never extend the old boundary to wait for a profit trigger. One purchase incurs
the same single allocated round-trip cost on its full quantity.

If several ordered closes become available at one clock, a structural breach
in that received prefix takes precedence over a profit trigger. If a profit
sale and scheduled expiry share an opening, record the time exit; the price
and quantity are the same. A previously scheduled structural sale retains
priority at that opening.

Compare this one new policy with both archived fixed-thirty and forecast-full
policies on the same original entries. Two baselines do not create two tuned
exit candidates. Report whole-calendar fills, settled known-subset returns,
known paired differences, both directions of changed outcome support, trigger
counts, sale reasons and date/name concentration. Keeping some profitable exits
does not establish positive expectation across losing or unsupported entries.

## Receipt, missingness and execution contract

Pending next-opening sales are processed before later observations. Time
expiry executes at its previously scheduled opening. Otherwise, a demanded
close missing after its original three-minute deadline or nonchronological
receipt leaves the holding result unresolved. A missing sale opening or a
sale quote not mature inside the continuous session remains unknown. New
earlier liquidation can finish before an old later gap, and a longer holding
period can encounter a new gap: report changed support explicitly rather than
subtracting aggregates from different known subsets.

The initial fill remains the original fill model, not an inference of queue
position. Cash remains nominal per-event cash; overlapping positions are not
a concurrently funded portfolio. Use exact rational arithmetic for prices,
quantities, cash and costs. No price/high hindsight optimizer or full-future
path input influences decisions. The future path is an outcome only.

## Ranking estimands fixed before new target joins

Report all sixteen fold-by-phase cells including unsupported cells. Within each
scored cell, weight dates equally, then symbol/session clusters equally within
date, then original episodes equally within cluster. Exclude same-cluster pairs.
Pair weights are products of row weights, normalized by eligible pair weight.

For each head report positive-versus-nonpositive pair AUC: the weighted fraction
of opposite-class pairs where the positive net outcome has higher prediction,
with prediction ties worth one half. One-class/no-comparable-pair cells remain
undefined. Also report continuous concordance: the fraction of unequal actual
net-value pairs whose predicted ordering agrees, prediction ties one half;
actual-value ties are excluded from its denominator and reported separately.
Calculate both statistics again using only same-session pairs. This separates
some across-day variation, but asynchronous clocks are not simultaneous choices.
Report support, signs, ties, pair counts and date/cluster counts. Exact target
and prediction comparisons preserve sign/ties; compensated floating statistical
weights and independent numerical tolerance do not convert cash to float.

For each cell delete each of eight NONOVERLAPPING consecutive five-date blocks
of its original forty-date calendar, recompute weights and both all-date and
same-date statistics. Report min/max and valid/undefined counts, preserving
empty original dates. These are sensitivity ranges, NOT confidence intervals,
p-values or dependence-adjusted significance. Constant scores give one half
where pairs exist; this is a ranking reference, not a trading baseline. Do not
select the best cell, score bin, head or threshold as a policy. No aggregate
may conceal the sixteen-cell table. Continuous concordance is not tau-b.

The official [AUC reference](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/generated/sklearn.metrics.roc_auc_score.html)
distinguishes ranking scores from binary decisions; the official
[concordance/tie definition](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.stats.kendalltau.html)
motivates explicit concordant, discordant and tied pairs. EO does not use the
latter's iid significance calculation or claim to compute its tau-b statistic.

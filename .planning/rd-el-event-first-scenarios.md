## Design decision and what changes

Start from a causal event and a known price level, not an inherited fixed
opening-hour screen. The three questions are failed downward progress after
a selloff, reclaim of a pre-known important low, and first pullback/resumption
after an upward impulse. Rules translate hypotheses into testable definitions;
they are our proposals, not authenticated numerical rules of a named trader.
Do not inherit EK's judgment minute, old limit price, structural stop window,
holding expiry, negative-hour mask or peer ranking. A new model name alone
does not establish new information or a new strategy.

Separate five objects: source support, causal situation, future path, possible
execution, and action conditional on a held position. A path that reached a
future high is not a trade filled there. More accurate forecasts can coexist
with worse execution and negative net payoff. Report these results separately.

4. Execution: compare the same selected events under separately frozen immediate

## Event design investigation, before support or outcome reads

An independent design review identified that requiring yesterday's low for B
would be narrower than the operator's request for an important pre-known low.
The initial B is therefore named **B_ORL30**, reclaim of the opening thirty-minute
range low. This is a new explicit within-session operational definition, not a
replacement presented as yesterday's low. Freeze the complete opening-range
low and its availability; only a breach bar starting strictly after the level
became available may start the event. Never update the level to later lows or
build it from an incomplete opening interval. Prior-day support remains a
separate measured input question and does not block this same-session question.

The initial numeric event contract to implement next uses completed minute
bars and a frozen pre-impulse volatility scale: the median true range of the
twenty scheduled bars ending before the impulse begins. The prior close needed
for each true range is also required. Missing/zero scale is unsupported.
The numbers below are proposed causal definitions, not a trader's verified
formula or a claim that the movement will exceed transaction cost.

| Hypothesis | Candidate | Confirmation / competing failure |
|---|---|---|
| A_DOWNFAIL | A three-minute close decline of at least three frozen scales; fix impulse low | Within minutes 3 through 12, a prior-two-bar retest near the fixed low followed by a close above those two highs; any earlier additional low extension beyond a quarter scale invalidates |
| B_ORL30 | Close below the already-known opening thirty-minute low by a quarter frozen scale | Close back above that same level by a quarter scale within twenty minutes; expiry and input gaps stay distinct |
| C_FIRSTPULLBACK | Five-minute close advance of at least three frozen scales | First half-scale close pullback from the running peak, then a close above the prior two highs within pullback minutes 2 through 10, while retaining at least half the advance |

For C, absence of a pullback within ten minutes is its own expiry. Freeze the
peak when the first pullback starts; do not choose the most attractive later
pullback. For A, the retest tolerance and maximum allowed new-low extension
are each a quarter frozen scale. A bar failing the structure overrides a
same-bar confirmation. Repeated candidates need a completed prior episode and
five scheduled minutes of reset. A/B/C may describe the same episode: preserve
overlap IDs and do not add their observations as independent evidence.

Before event access, a new source registration will resolve exact equality,
episode endpoints, available-time calculations and comparison policies through
invented positive/negative/boundary examples. The support-only registration
does not authorize running these detectors. During the first event census,
report past impulse magnitude relative to the fixed transaction cost and
volatility, even when small. This distinguishes an economically small context
from a failed predictive model without optimizing a threshold on later profits.

Case cards will include onset, every permitted later decision and the facts
that changed the state. Forecasts must be possible before confirmation; do not
reduce the whole question to ranking only events already confirmed. Candidate
base rates, waiting for confirmation and model-selected entry will be separate
controls on the same causal candidate roster. Post-entry hold/reduce/exit is a
separate matched-entry comparison. This defines a testable succession of
decisions rather than an unconstrained narrative about what a chart meant.

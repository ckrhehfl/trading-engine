## Comparison design investigated, not yet an executable family

The minimum useful question is the incremental selection value at matched
formation-time opportunity counts, not the separate effect of investing more
capital when the activity filter is removed. A provisional control removes
only the activity threshold from the same historical eligible source population.
Its count ceiling comes from ACTIVITY's pre-opening target declarations for
each formation, never its successful fills or execution-session sale outcomes.
Use the existing fixed seed/hash ordering and retain the control's own held-code,
slot, cash and failed-fill constraints. Do not substitute targets after a failed
fill or re-normalize a small selection to full investment. ACTIVITY issues may
naturally appear in the unfiltered control.

A single frozen hash control is a descriptive fixed portfolio, not a
permutation distribution or randomization test. Count matching does not ensure
equal realized cash exposure, sectors, momentum, volatility, delivery or holding
duration. Report these limitations and distinguish price returns from dividend-
inclusive returns. Multiple controls would measure assignment sensitivity, not
create new independent market observations. A broader unrestricted-slot
control answers a separate allocation-policy question and is not an implicit
additional trial. Final family scope is frozen only after the sizing decision.

Source inspection established that AQ/AR/BL preserve the complete formation
population, but the BL normal-period and BM price packages cover only activity
survivors. BI numeric failures prove the activity condition fails; they do not
prove control eligibility. Neither the survivor list, the holding-price panel,
nor explicit negative-type controls may define the comparator population.
The existing BI snapshot retains normal-date/state/turnover metadata for the
broader potentially eligible population and can avoid a fresh activity DB read.
Historical operating periods and consequential control holding/action coverage
still need positive evidence, using existing saved issuer sources first.

After sizing permits the next step, retain the full source population and
resolve result-independent hash-prefix candidates that could affect target
selection. Unknown earlier candidates cannot be skipped. Freeze the exact
potential holding and successor union before price/action input consumption;
reuse the existing accounting engine. Do not require investigation of names
whose verified exclusions or hash positions cannot affect this frozen control.
